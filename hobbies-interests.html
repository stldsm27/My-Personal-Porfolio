Cooking

I enjoy cooking for my family, especially since it's easy to prepare a delicious meal without needing too many tools.

Since there are so many recipes available on the internet, I like to experiment and add my own personality to the dishes I make by tweaking the recipes to suit my taste.

Cooking is practical, fun, and gives me immediate satisfaction when everyone enjoys the meal.

Baking

Baking is a bit more demanding because it requires careful measurements and several tools that need to be cleaned afterwards.

However, though it's tiring sometimes, it’s truly satisfying to create a beautifully baked and delicious good.

I love the precision and patience it requires, and seeing a perfect cake or pastry makes it all worth it.

Reading Novels

I love reading novels in my free time.

I usually use my Kindle, which is convenient and helps me avoid allergies from the smell of physical books while still enjoying a good story.

Reading allows me to relax and explore new worlds without leaving home.

FUN FACT!

I used to bake simply as a hobby because I enjoyed creating sweet treats for my family.

Over time, my family and friends loved my baked goods so much that they encouraged me to sell them occasionally.

Baking is precise and a bit tiring, but it’s truly satisfying to create something delicious!
